\subsection{Image registration and preprocessing}
Raw images were cropped to the bounding box of the inscription.
Ground truth images were then downscaled to the individual scales of their respective raw counterparts, and aligned to the raw images in Krita by translation, rotation, and scaling, using the raw images as background reference.
